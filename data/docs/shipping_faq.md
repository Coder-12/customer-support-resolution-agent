# Shipping FAQ

## Delayed Shipment
Standard shipments may take 3 to 7 business days after fulfillment. Delays can occur due to carrier processing, weather, address verification, or inventory transfer.

## Tracking Not Updating
Tracking updates may be delayed for up to 48 hours after a carrier receives the package. If tracking has not updated after 48 hours, check the order status and carrier handoff logs before escalating.

## Escalation
Escalate to Logistics if the package has no tracking movement for more than 5 business days, the delivery address is invalid, or the carrier reports an exception.
