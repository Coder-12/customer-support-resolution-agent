# Payment Failure FAQ

## Common Causes
Payments can fail due to insufficient funds, expired cards, bank declines, address verification mismatch, or temporary payment processor outages.

## Recommended Steps
Ask the customer to verify card details, billing address, available balance, and whether their bank blocked the transaction. If there is a known processor issue, acknowledge the problem and ask the customer to retry later.

## Escalation
Escalate to Billing Operations if multiple payment methods fail, the customer was charged but the order did not complete, or payment logs show processor errors.
