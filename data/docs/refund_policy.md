# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers are eligible for a refund when the purchase is within the refund window and the product or service terms allow cancellation. Refund eligibility may depend on subscription status, usage, and prior refund history.

## Processing Timeline
After a refund is approved, it is usually initiated within 1 business day. Most banks and card networks post approved refunds within 5 to 10 business days. Some payment methods may take longer depending on the bank or payment processor.

## Customer Messaging
Support engineers should avoid promising an exact arrival date unless a payment processor confirmation exists. If the refund is still inside the normal processing window, explain the expected timeline and ask the customer to check again after the window has passed.

## Escalation
Escalate to Billing Operations when the refund has been approved for more than 10 business days, when the order ID is missing and cannot be verified, or when payment processor status is unavailable.
