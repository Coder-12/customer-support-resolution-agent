# Subscription Cancellation

## Cancellation Behavior
Customers can cancel subscriptions from account settings. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not automatically refund the current billing period unless refund policy criteria are met.

## Customer Messaging
Confirm that cancellation prevents future charges. Do not state that the customer will receive a refund unless the refund policy and billing records support it.

## Escalation
Escalate to Billing Operations if the customer reports duplicate charges, cannot access account settings, or requests a refund outside standard policy.
