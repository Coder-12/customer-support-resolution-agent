# Billing Disputes

## Duplicate Charges
Duplicate-charge reports require transaction review before a customer-facing resolution is promised. Support engineers should verify the order ID, customer ID, charge timestamps, and payment processor status before stating whether a charge is valid, pending, duplicated, or refundable.

## Customer Messaging
Do not promise an immediate refund or credit for duplicate charges until Billing Operations confirms the processor records. Acknowledge the concern, explain that duplicate charges require transaction verification, and route the case with the available payment identifiers.

## Escalation
Escalate to Billing Operations when a customer reports being charged twice, when two payment records appear for one renewal period, or when the order/payment lookup fails.
